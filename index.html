Gem Maze

Collect every 💎 to unlock the exit, then escape as fast as you can. The mazes get bigger, beetles start crawling around, and from level 6 the lights go out!

  • Keys: arrow keys or WASD (hold to keep moving)
  • Touch: swipe to zoom down a hallway, or use the arrow pad
  • Beetles: bumping one costs you 5 seconds
  • Stars: finish faster for more ⭐
